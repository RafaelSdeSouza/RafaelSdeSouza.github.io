About Rafael

I am an astrophysicist at the University of Hertfordshire and an Adjunct Associate Professor at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill. I was trained in astronomy in Brazil, at UFRJ and the University of São Paulo.

Research has since taken me to Japan, South Korea, Hungary, the United States and China. I founded the Cosmostatistics Initiative in 2014 and co-authored Bayesian Models for Astrophysical Data. I also write science fiction.
