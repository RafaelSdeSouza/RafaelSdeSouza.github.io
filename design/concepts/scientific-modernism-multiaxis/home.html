Rafael S. de Souza

Astrophysicist

Statistical inference from astronomical observations affected by selection, inaccessible likelihoods, lossy representations, and spatial dependence.

Applications include Population III star formation, cosmological cluster counts, Milky Way structure, resolved galaxy spectroscopy, transient follow-up, and thermonuclear reaction rates.

Senior Lecturer, University of Hertfordshire · Adjunct Associate Professor, University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill

Research

Current questions

Incomplete calibration, representation completion, path-based geometry, and resolved galaxy structure.

Incomplete data

What remains identifiable when a calibration sample omits part of the target population?

Current work derives bounds on population quantities when additional spectroscopy cannot recover missing colour–magnitude support.

Representations

Does a summary retain the variation required by a later physical parameter?

Controlled experiments distinguish the variance captured by a representation from the information needed by the scientific estimand.

Path-based geometry

How should radial structure be defined for irregular sources with multiple centres?

RadialPaths measures distance through the observed footprint and retains the association between each location and candidate centres.

Resolved galaxies

Which regions of a galaxy remain coherent in spectra or multiband SEDs?

CAPIVARA and SAGUI group neighbouring measurements by spectral information without imposing a bulge–disc decomposition.

Selected work

Selected Contributions

Four results from 2015–2025 span statistical models, a methods monograph, Galactic star formation, and IFU segmentation.

2015 · Statistical models

Generalized linear models in astronomy

Binary events, positive continuous measurements, and overdispersed counts were treated with sampling laws appropriate to each observable.

2017 · Book

Bayesian Models for Astrophysical Data

A 408-page practical synthesis connected generalized, hierarchical, and likelihood-free models to complete astronomical analyses. The book received the 2018 PROSE Award in Cosmology & Astronomy.

2021 · Galactic structure

SPICY and the Sagittarius arm

A catalogue of roughly 120,000 candidate young stellar objects supported the identification of 25 star-forming regions in a narrow structure about 1 kpc long.

2025 · Resolved galaxies

CAPIVARA

Spectral similarity grouped IFU spaxels into regions coherent in continuum and emission-line properties without imposing a bulge–disc decomposition.
