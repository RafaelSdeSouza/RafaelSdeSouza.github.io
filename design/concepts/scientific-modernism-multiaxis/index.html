Art direction

Scientific modernism, with evidence in view.

Three related directions retain the current navy, cobalt, serif–sans hierarchy, and archival clarity. They differ only in where authentic imagery, scientific figures, and literary material enter the composition.

Direction A · recommended

Atlas / contour

The existing typographic system gains a stronger atlas logic: a wide reading column, precise marginal metadata, and large authentic figures with captions. Original artwork remains concentrated in the homepage hero.

Research

Current questions

Use: Home, Research, Selected Contributions. Character: contemporary scientific monograph; asymmetry follows image and text content.

Direction B

Archival plate

Figures behave like plates in a monograph. Pages use a stricter baseline, shorter headings, and denser bibliographic detail. Decoration is nearly absent.

Figure · caption · source

Use: Publications and Software. Character: maximally functional and durable, but too reserved for the whole site.

Direction C

Literary margin

A narrow outer margin can hold a verified work attribution or a short excerpt from Rafael’s own writing. It is strongest when confined to Writing and used once.

Recommendation

One system, different levels of expression

Use Atlas / contour as the common system. Apply Archival plate discipline to Publications and Software. Reserve Literary margin for one verified excerpt on Writing. This creates variation without giving separate pages unrelated visual identities.
