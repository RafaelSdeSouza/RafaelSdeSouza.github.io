Art direction

Scientific modernism, with evidence in view.

Three related directions retain the current navy, cobalt, serif–sans hierarchy, and archival clarity. They differ only in where authentic imagery, scientific figures, and literary material enter the composition.

Direction A · recommended

Atlas / contour

The existing typographic system gains a stronger atlas logic: a wide reading column, precise marginal metadata, and large authentic figures with captions. Original artwork remains concentrated in the homepage hero.

Direction B

Archival plate

Figures behave like plates in a monograph. Pages use a stricter baseline, shorter headings, and denser bibliographic detail. Decoration is nearly absent.

Source-derived geometry

Three restrained motifs

These are identity cues, not plotted results. Their source and permitted use remain explicit so that an abstraction is never mistaken for evidence.

A · Centre-conditioned path

Use the existing mark or a single thin centre-to-centre path beside relevant Research content. Do not redraw it as a site logo.

B · Ordered spectral trace

A continuous line with sparse ordered points. Use only near spectropath or representation work; never as a generic site divider.

C · Segmentation boundary

Two quiet boundary traces can frame a CAPIVARA figure. They must not be presented as extracted measurements.
