Projects and Software

Research software.

Methods become useful when they can be tested, reused, and inspected. These projects turn statistical ideas into tools for real astronomical data.

Galaxy imaging

SAGUI

SED-based segmentation for multi-band galaxy images, designed to use colour and morphology together in deep-field data such as JADES.

IFU analysis

Capivara

Spectral-based segmentation for IFU data cubes, separating galaxy structures by their spectral fingerprints rather than by light alone.

Median-based radial Fourier power spectra for compact, reproducible summaries of spatial structure in astronomical images.
