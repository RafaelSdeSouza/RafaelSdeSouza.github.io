COIN

Cosmostatistics Initiative

Founded 2014

I founded COIN in 2014 for joint research on astronomical and statistical problems. At its residences, small groups live together and work on a few research projects.

COIN is co-led by Emille E. O. Ishida, Alberto Krone-Martins and me.
